Week 7 · Tools, sunrise alarm, Showcase + full phone remote for Netflix/YouTube new 19 Nov 2026

  • Swap button on the wallpaper: a small Swap button at the bottom of Layer 1 swaps the screens straight away (on one screen it says Dashboard).
  • Netflix / YouTube open without a tap: tap an app on your phone and it opens full screen on the PC.
  • Control everything from the phone (after the one-time setup below): Home → Remote card. Remote = arrow pad + OK to move around rows and open titles · Touchpad = drag a pointer, tap to click, two fingers to scroll · Type = type into the search box, or tap "Search Netflix". Plus play/pause, ±10 s, scrub, volume, mute, full screen, Skip intro / Skip ad, close.
  • Tools tab (laptop and phone): Polls (one vote each, can close on a timer) · Laundry timers (everyone gets a chime when the wash is done) · Split & Settle (add what you paid; it works out who pays whom, with a Pay with UPI button that opens GPay/PhonePe/Paytm with the amount filled in) · Sunrise alarm (the wall slowly fills with warm light before your alarm, then chimes softly).
  • Showcase (V, or the button next to Night): the wall turns into a ring of light that dances to the music in the room (laptop microphone; nothing is recorded or sent).
  • The wall screen also shows running timers and the newest poll.
One-time setup for the phone remote: on the laptop open Chrome → chrome://extensions → turn on Developer mode (top right) → Load unpacked → Linux files → jharokha → extension → Select. Done: "Jharokha Remote" connects by itself.
Try: Tools → Sunrise alarm → Test now (a 20-second sunrise). Then add your UPI ID in Split & Settle.

Fixes · Night Watch + phones that can't connect built 18 Nov 2026

  • New Night Watch clock: a soft deep-red serif clock (Fraunces), AM/PM, a thin minute bar and the date. It drifts a few pixels every minute so nothing burns in.
  • Easy to wake: tap or click anywhere, or press any key. Moving the mouse brings the cursor back with a "tap to wake" hint; the cursor only hides while idle.
  • Share over the internet (for when the Wi-Fi blocks phones): one switch in Connect a phone gives a free Cloudflare https link that works on any network, even mobile data. Strangers only see the join page until you approve them. Off by default.
To turn it on: in the Linux terminal run npm run tunnel:install once (asks for sudo). Then Home → People → Connect a phone → Share over the internet… → Yes, share it. Scan the new QR with your phone.

Week 4 · Access: friends join from their phone built 18 Nov 2026

  • Join from a phone: scan the QR (or type laptop-ip:8080/join), enter a name, then wait for approval.
  • Approval tray: "Priya wants to join" pops up on the dashboard. Tap ✓ Collaborator, Viewer or Owner, or ✕ to say no. Over the wallpaper a small 🔔 pill appears instead.
  • Device token: an approved phone stays signed in for 30 days. Remove it any time from the Access card.
  • Kill switch (owner-only mode), quiet hours (friends read-only at night), audit log, rate limits, max 20 devices.
  • This laptop is now paired as an owner. If the dashboard ever says "Pair this screen", run npm run pair in the Linux terminal.
  • Fixed: the QR now has the proper white border, so phone cameras can lock on.
Try it: On your phone (same Wi-Fi, not mobile data), open the browser and type http://192.168.10.69:8080/join. If it loads, ask to join and approve yourself on the laptop. If it doesn't load: check Settings → Linux → Port forwarding → 8080 is on. If it still fails, the hostel Wi-Fi probably blocks phone-to-laptop traffic (then a phone hotspot or travel router is the fix).

Week 3 · Server core built 18 Nov 2026

  • New server (Fastify + live WebSocket + SQLite) runs by itself in Linux and restarts if it crashes.
  • Vitals Ring: one circle for Power · CPU · Network · Server. Each part turns amber or red and says why.
  • Server panel: uptime, traffic, devices, database, disk, memory and errors, live every 2 s.
  • Live QR for phones. Press Set Wi-Fi IP once (Linux can't see the laptop's Wi-Fi address).
  • "Server offline" badge if Linux stops. The wallpaper keeps playing, and the badge clears by itself when the server is back.
Try it: Open Jharokha → Start → ▣ Dashboard (or S). Look at the ring and the Server card. Set your Wi-Fi IP (Settings → Network → Wi-Fi) and scan the QR with your phone; port forwarding for 8080 must be on (Settings → Linux → Port forwarding).
